Government Measure for Tourism Management 2024 - 2027
For years, Barcelona City Council has prioritized the comprehensive management and governance of tourism as an essential element to guarantee a balanced and sustainable city and improve the integration of tourism into the city.
High-traffic Areas (HTAs) are urban areas with a high concentration of residents and visitors. Barcelona City Council has launched a set of actions and measures to address the specific challenges of each territory.
Barcelona Destination Tourism Sustainability Plan (PSTD)
The City Council has developed the Barcelona Destination Tourism Sustainability Plan (PSTD) to respond to the 4 main axes defined by the European Union itself: the green and sustainable transition, energy efficiency, digital transition and competitiveness.
Barcelona, a benchmark city in tourism
Urban tourism has become a global phenomenon in recent years that also affects our city. The visitor economy employs more than 163,000 people, represents around 13% of GDP and generates annual spending of more than 10 billion euros.
Barcelona City Council is working on a strategy to manage tourist activity, promote territorial deconcentration, diversify the offer and visitor profile, commit to strategic promotion and promote balance with everyday life.

Mayors and representatives of major European cities meet in Barcelona to strengthen the urban voice in European Union policies
Barcelona hosted the meeting of the Eurocities Political Executive Committee on 28 and 29 October. The city currently holds the network’s vice-presidency. The meeting coincided with the Eurocities Economic Development Forum and addressed the network’s political priorities, the role of cities in the future EU budget and support for Ukrainian municipalities.
The transformation of La Sagrera brings Barcelona into the international “Pioneering Places” programme
The city has joined an international network that promotes more sustainable, inclusive and resilient urban development, with the transformation of La Sagrera serving as a flagship project.
